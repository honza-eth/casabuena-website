Nine steps.
One contact.

Book the whole way, or just the steps you need. We stay in touch with the exporter throughout, so you always know where the car is and what is happening to it.

  1. 01

    Leaving China

    The exporter loads the car and the ship sails. That is where the exporter’s job ends and ours begins. We collect the commercial invoice, packing list and car documents and check that they match.

  2. 02

    At sea

    During the voyage the shipping line issues the bill of lading (B/L). We make sure the exporter releases it in time, chase anything missing and book the port, transport and clearance in advance.

  3. 03

    The port

    The port is whichever makes sense at the time: Rotterdam, Hamburg, Bremerhaven or Gdańsk. There we deal with the shipping line and the terminal: arrival, charges and the container release. Without the release, the container cannot leave the port.

  4. 04

    Onward transport

    Under customs supervision, the container can travel on to any EU country you choose. Duty and VAT are then paid where the cars are cleared.

  5. 05

    Out of the container

    Before clearance we unload the cars and get them ready for customs. This is where a container can be split, so each car can go its own way.

  6. 06

    Customs

    At the port or in the EU country you choose, with a customs broker we trust, picked case by case. You usually remain the importer. It’s the cleanest route, and we prepare the paperwork.

  7. 07

    Homologation

    We supply the technical documentation and get the car approved in the country where it will be registered.

  8. 08

    Localisation

    On request: where the model allows it, we switch the system to English or another language and set up European apps.

  9. 09

    Registration and handover

    We register the car in the EU country you choose and hand it over with plates. Dealers can take cars cleared and approved, ready to register.
